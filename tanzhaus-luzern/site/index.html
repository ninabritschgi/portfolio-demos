everybody dances here.

Classes for children, teens and adults, from beginners to experienced dancers and aspiring professionals.

For Children & Teens

From first steps to serious pre-professional training, our Youth Program gives young dancers the skills, confidence, and support to thrive in an encouraging space.

explore youth classes

For Adults

Starting fresh? Coming back? Never really stopped?
Our adult classes meet you where you are. With flexible booking, you can choose the classes you love and dance when it works for you. Great teaching, no unnecessary semester commitment.

explore adult classes
Why Tanzhaus?
Teaching is personal.
Our teachers know their students. Small classes, individual feedback and plenty of attention to help you progress.
Dance keeps evolving. So do we.
We respect where dance comes from while staying curious about where it's going. Our teaching keeps learning, too.
Every body is welcome.
Diverse styles, diverse ages, diverse bodies. We're building a school where everyone has a place to enjoy dance.
There is more than one way to be a dancer.
Some of us dance once a week. Some of us train every day. Everybody dances here.
