Pre-professional program

Individually tailored training and mentorship for dancers ready to take things further.  Whether you're aiming for a professional career, a place in a professional institution or simply want focused, high-level training, we'll build a program around where you want to go.

Audition for the programBook a consultation

Who is this program for?

  • Kunst & Sport (K&S) students, ages 12–20
  • Dedicated teens
  • Motivated adults
  • Aspiring professionals
  • Dancers seeking focused, high-level training

What you get.

  • A customisable program built around your goals
  • Professional creation and performance experience
  • Your choice of ballet or contemporary focus
  • A genuine dance community
  • A balanced school-and-dance schedule
  • An encouraging, team-oriented environment
  • A strong technical foundation

Choose your Focus

Ballet or contemporary — your call.

Every dancer's path is different. Choose the focus that fits your goals, and we'll work with you to build a program that makes sense for your body, your life and your ambitions.

How to join

Our Pre-Professional Program is invite-only — every dancer joins through a successful audition. Here's how it works:
1.
Submit your online application.
Tell us about yourself through our online application form. Your background, your goals, your training so far.
2.
Audition.
We hold auditions on a set date — apply ahead of time to secure your spot. If the date doesn't work for you, get in touch and we'll arrange a private audition.
Next audition date: TBA
3.
Train with us.
Successful auditionees are invited to join the program. From there, we build your individual training plan with you — ballet or contemporary focus, daily lessons, conditioning, mental coaching, and the rest.
start your application

Daily life in the program

What the day-to-day actually looks like:
  • Daily dance lessons — consistent, high-quality training
  • A personalised schedule, including rest days — because recovery is part of the work
  • Strength and conditioning — building the body that supports the artistry
  • Mental coaching — resilience, focus and confidence, on and off the stage
  • Work alongside professionals who are currently active in the industry

Where our dancers go

Our former students have gone on to:
  • Star as the lead in Billy Elliot
  • Study BA Dance at ZHdK
  • Pursue professional ballet studies at TaZ (Tanz Akademie Zürich)
  • Pursue professional ballet studies at Dutch National Ballet Academy in Amsterdam, NL
  • Study BA Dance at Codarts in Rotterdam, NL
  • Study BA Fine Arts at ZHdK
