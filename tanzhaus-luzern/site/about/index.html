Our Story

Tanzhaus Luzern is one of Central Switzerland's largest dance schools — but we don't think bigger is the important part. What matters is what happens inside the studio.
Long before Tanzhaus Luzern existed, people were already dancing here. The space has been home to dance studios since the 1980s, with different schools, teachers and generations of dancers shaping its story over the years. In 2023, The Dance Center closed its doors. Rather than letting this much-loved space fall silent, Tanzhaus Luzern was founded, and a new chapter began.

Today, we continue the space's long history while looking towards the future. Tanzhaus Luzern brings together dancers of all ages and ambitions — from first steps and weekly hobby classes to pre-professional training. The names have changed. The studios have evolved. But one thing has remained the same: this house is a space for dance.

What we believe

Everyone starts somewhere.
No one is too old, too new, or too anything to dance. The first step is the hardest — we make it easy.
Teaching that keeps learning.
Our teachers are dancers and educators. We stay curious, keep learning and adapt our teaching as knowledge evolves.
Challenge with support
Safe, healthy, non-judgmental training, grounded in professional experience, ongoing learning, and a thoughtful approach to dance education.
There is more than one way to be a dancer.
Hobby dancers and aspiring professionals don't need the same goals to share the same floor. Everyone matters here.

Meet the team

Our international team of teachers are trained as both dancers and educators, with extensive stage experience behind them.
Olivia Lecomte
founder, managing director & teacher
Ballet, Contemporary, & Repertoire
After training at Canada’s National Ballet School and Codarts University of the Arts (BA Dance Performance), Olivia began her professional stage career dancing for the Tanz Luzerner Theater. She then freelanced for renowned companies in Europe and has been teaching in Luzern since 2017.

Alexandra Andreeva-Stadler
Teacher
Ballet & Repertoire
Alexandra studied dance at the Vaganova Academy in Bulgaria and later in Switzerland before going on to dance professionally for many European companies. Parallel to her stage career, Alexandra earned a master’s degree in ballet education and directing.
Andrea Günthör
Teacher
Kindertanz
Originally from Lucerne Andrea has an extensive dance education which include The Royal Academy of Dancing, London and the Ballettberufsschule, Zürich. As a dancer she has performed with some of the major Swiss dance institutions such as Stadttheater Bern, Luzerner Theater, Theater St.Gallen, amongst others. Also passionate about teaching she has taught in Zurich and Luzern and since 2021 she is part of the Tanzhaus Luzern staff. She is also a certified trainer in Alexander Technik, Pilates and Yoga Teacher, Level I.
Aurora Andreeva-Bobbià

Aurora trained as a dancer both at the Vaganova Academy in Bulgaria and later in Switzerland before performing with Valery Panov’s Ballet Theatre. She then earned a BA in Philosophy with a specialization in Dance as well as an MA in Choreography and Stage Direction in her native Bulgaria.
